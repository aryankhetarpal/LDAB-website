Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers for parents and students.

Academics

Will ballet affect my child's studies?

Ballet builds focus, discipline and time management. Many of our students balance classes alongside school and college academics.

Do students have to take examinations?

No. Examinations are optional and offered alongside regular training, based on the student's level and readiness.

Future & Careers

Can ballet lead to a career?

Yes. Through internationally recognised examinations and teacher training, dancers can pursue performance and teaching paths.

Can older students become instructors?

Yes. Dedicated dancers can grow into teaching roles at LDAB and pursue our Teacher Training programme.
