Annual Awards & Recognition

At LDAB, we believe every milestone deserves to be celebrated. Each year, we host an Annual Awards Ceremony to recognise and honour the dedication, progress, achievements, and commitment of our students throughout the year.

Examination Achievements

Students who appear for examinations are recognised based on their results:

ISTD

Students who achieve a Distinction are honoured with a trophy. All other students who successfully complete their examinations receive medals celebrating their achievement and grade.

CSTD

Students who achieve Honours or Honours Plus are honoured with a trophy. All other students who successfully complete their examinations receive medals in recognition of their accomplishment.

UNESCO

Students who complete the relevant certification programme receive an official certificate from the UNESCO Academy.

Special Recognition

Celebrating dedication and commitment beyond examination results.

Beyond examination results, LDAB also celebrates students who demonstrate exceptional dedication and commitment throughout the year. Students achieving milestones such as 100% attendance, remarkable consistency, outstanding effort, or exceptional dedication to their training may be awarded a medal and certificate from the academy.

Competition Achievements

How awards earned at competitions are recognised and retained.

Solo Performances

Students who participate in dance competitions are encouraged to celebrate and retain the awards they earn through solo performances.

Duo, Trio & Group Performances

For duo, trio, and group performances, the competition trophy received is retained by the academy as part of its collective achievements and legacy. The participating students are subsequently presented with a trophy or medal by LDAB, ensuring that their contribution and achievement are personally recognised and celebrated.
