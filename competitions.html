Competitions

Students who demonstrate the necessary technical ability and performance readiness may be encouraged to participate in local, national, and international ballet competitions.

What Competitions Offer

Gain Stage Experience

Real performance conditions that studio classes alone can't fully replicate.

Develop Resilience

Learning to handle pressure, setbacks and sportsmanship with grace.

Professional Feedback

Adjudication from experienced judges sharpens technique and artistry.

Celebrate Excellence

Recognising the discipline and artistry behind every performance.

Participation is always based on teacher recommendation and student readiness.

Competitions We've Participated In

A few competitions LDAB students have taken part in over the years.

APDC

The Asia Pacific Dance Competition (APDC) is a regional competition held across cities in the Asia Pacific, bringing together dancers from multiple countries to compete, perform and be adjudicated by international judges.

Explore More

BTP

Born to Perform (BTP) is a CSTD regional dance competition that brings together students across classical, contemporary and performance categories to compete and be assessed by qualified adjudicators.

Explore More

FIBC

The Frankfurt International Ballet Competition (FIBC) is an international ballet competition for children and young non-professional dancers, offering participants access to an experienced, internationally renowned jury.

Explore More

IAF

The International Arts Festival (IAF), organised by the Singapore Dance Alliance, is open to dancers of all nationalities across classical ballet, contemporary, cultural dance and more — competing as soloists, duos, trios and groups.

Explore More

IYDC

The International Youth Dance Competition (IYDC) is brought to you by International Youth Dance Company. This event is set to encourage young aspiring dancers from across the Globe to hone their skills and to showcase their talent through a series of workshops and dance competitions.

Explore More
