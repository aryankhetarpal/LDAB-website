Meet Our Founder & Artistic Director

Neha Suhjani was introduced to classical ballet by a Russian ballerina at a very young age. Her love for the art form took her to Bangalore, where she began training in a more structured way — and, since formal dance examinations were uncommon in India at the time, on to Kuala Lumpur, where she completed her ISTD graded exams under Ms Rachel Aw and later her ISTD Teacher's exams under Ms Suraya Kamil. She pursued dance purely out of love, never originally planning to take it up professionally.

A Chartered Accountant by profession, Neha left a promising career at one of the most prestigious companies to pursue her passion for dance and children, founding LDAB in 2016 to build a dance community in Pune. Her performance credits span national and international stages, and she has choreographed and taught students both within India and abroad, continuing to shape the next generation of aspiring ballet dancers.

Neha has completed numerous examinations and taken part in countless competitions and performances across the Asia Pacific region. Her examination work and study of the ISTD/CSTD syllabus give her deep experience across Jazz, Classical Ballet and Contemporary — and her infectious love of dance shapes LDAB's belief that ballet should be fun and accessible to everyone.

  • ISTD Graded & Teacher's Certifications (Kuala Lumpur)
  • Trained across the ISTD/CSTD syllabus — Jazz, Classical Ballet & Contemporary
  • Former Chartered Accountant
  • Founder & Artistic Director, Les Danseuses Academy of Ballet (est. 2016)

Our History & Experience

Les Danseuses Academy of Ballet was founded in 2016 by Neha Suhjani, a Chartered Accountant who gave up a promising corporate career to build a dance community in Pune. What began as one woman's love for classical ballet — discovered as a child and later formalised through ISTD graded and Teacher's examinations in Kuala Lumpur — has grown into a full academy shaped by the same discipline and care from day one.

Since then, LDAB's examination work and study of the ISTD/CSTD syllabus has taken students across Jazz, Classical Ballet and Contemporary, with performance and competition credits spanning India and the wider Asia Pacific region. Students train and examine following internationally recognised syllabi, carrying the same foundations they build here into every stage, exam and performance that follows.
